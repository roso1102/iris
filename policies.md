# IRIS - Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to IRIS (Document Intelligence Platform), operated by Procambrian. This document outlines our Terms of Service (ToS) and Privacy Policy. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to these terms and acknowledge our data collection practices.

## 2. Infrastructure & Compliance
IRIS is architected natively on enterprise-grade infrastructure (Google Cloud Platform and Qdrant). As a result, our platform inherits the highest global standards for security, availability, and privacy. 

Our underlying infrastructure maintains compliance with:
- **SOC 1, SOC 2 Type II, and SOC 3**
- **ISO/IEC 27001, 27017, and 27018** (Information Security & Cloud Privacy)
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation)
- **CCPA/CPRA** (California Consumer Privacy Laws)
- **HIPAA and FedRAMP High** (Available for specialized deployments)

## 3. What Data We Collect
To provide enterprise-grade document intelligence and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), we collect, process, and store the following data:

### A. Account & Authentication Data 
*Managed via Google Firebase Authentication & Firestore.*
- **Identifiers:** Email addresses and unique User UIDs.
- **Security:** Passwords (securely hashed and managed entirely by Firebase; we never see plaintext passwords).
- **Authorization:** Tenant IDs (Workspace identifiers) and User Roles (e.g., admin, member) stored as Custom Claims and in our Firestore database for role-based access control (RBAC).

### B. User-Uploaded Content & Documents
*Managed via Google Cloud Storage (GCS) and Qdrant.*
- **Original Files:** PDF documents and other supported file types uploaded to the platform, stored securely in GCS.
- **Processed Text:** Extracted text, parsed layouts, tables, and OCR data processed by our ingestion pipeline (Docling and Gemini Vision).
- **Vector Data:** Mathematical embeddings (generated by Vertex AI `text-embedding-004`) representing the content of your documents, stored in the Qdrant vector database for semantic search.
- **Visual Assets:** Cropped images of figures or tables extracted from your documents for visual question answering.

### C. Chat & Session Data 
*Managed via Firestore.*
- **Queries:** The text of all questions, queries, and prompts you submit to the IRIS assistant.
- **Session History:** The platform retains a sliding window of recent chat messages (e.g., the last 6 interactions) in Firestore to provide conversational context and memory.
- **Generated Output:** The synthesized answers and citations generated by the LLM in response to your queries.
- **Metadata:** Session IDs, timestamps, and active document IDs associated with your chats.

### D. System & Security Logs
*Managed via Cloud Logging & Firestore.*
- **Telemetry & Performance:** API request times, document ingestion progress, model inference durations, and system performance metrics.
- **Security Logs:** We log failed authentication attempts and queries that trigger prompt injection guardrails or violate safety policies.

## 4. How We Process & Secure Your Data
- **Encryption:** All user data, documents, and chat logs are encrypted **at rest** and **in transit** (via HTTPS/TLS).
- **No Outside Processing:** All data processing, storage, and model inference happen entirely within secure cloud boundaries. We do not send your data to external consumer APIs.
- **Multi-Tenant Isolation:** Your data is strictly isolated using cryptographic `tenant_id` boundaries. Users in one tenant mathematically cannot access documents, embeddings, or chat sessions belonging to another tenant.
- **Model Interaction:** Your document text and queries are passed to Google Vertex AI models strictly for the purpose of generating answers to your questions. 

## 5. The "No Training" Guarantee
**We do not use your proprietary documents, user data, or chat logs to train foundational AI models.** All interactions with our LLMs (Vertex AI) are bound by enterprise data privacy agreements, ensuring your proprietary data remains yours.

## 6. Data Deletion and Retention
- **Cascading Deletions:** When you delete a document from IRIS, the system automatically triggers a cascading deletion. This definitively removes the original file from GCS, the metadata/text records from Firestore, and the vector embeddings from Qdrant. No orphaned data is left behind.
- **Account Deletion:** You may request account deletion at any time, which will wipe your Firebase Auth profile and associated user metadata.

## 7. Acceptable Use Policy
- **Authorized Access:** You may only access the platform using accounts provisioned or authorized by your tenant administrator.
- **Prohibited Conduct:** You agree not to attempt to bypass security controls, manipulate system prompts (e.g., prompt injection or "DAN" attacks), reverse-engineer the platform, or attempt to access data belonging to other tenants.
- **Intellectual Property:** You retain all ownership rights to the documents you upload. We claim no ownership over your proprietary data.

## 8. Modifications
We reserve the right to modify these policies as the IRIS platform evolves. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of any updated terms.
